# Business

Client-facing and commercial deliverables: profiles, decks, sales material, and priced offers.

## Folders

| Folder | Owns | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [company-profile/](company-profile/) | Company profile documents | Planned v1.1 |
| [presentations/](presentations/) | All slide-based deliverables | Planned v1.1 |
| [sales/](sales/) | Sales narrative and collateral | Planned v1.1 |
| [proposal/](proposal/) | Priced, scoped client offers | Planned v1.1 |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> `presentations` owns every slide deck. `proposal` owns anything priced and scoped. The other two link to them rather than defining their own.
